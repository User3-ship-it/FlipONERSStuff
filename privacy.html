Privacy Policy for Apes

Effective Date: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the virtual reality game Apes, developed and published by FlipONER.

This policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how players can contact the developer regarding privacy concerns.

Information We Collect

Apes may collect limited information required for gameplay and platform functionality, including:

  • Voice chat audio when microphone permissions are granted
  • User-selected media files for mixed reality or LIV features
  • Basic gameplay and device information required for multiplayer services and stability

Microphone Usage

Apes uses the device microphone to support in-game voice chat and communication features during multiplayer gameplay.

Media Permissions

Apes may request access to images, videos, and audio files stored on the device for mixed reality recording, LIV integration, screenshots, recordings, and user-selected media features.

The application only accesses media files explicitly selected or used by the player.

How Information Is Used

Information collected by the application is used solely for:

  • Supporting multiplayer gameplay features
  • Providing voice communication functionality
  • Supporting mixed reality and LIV recording features
  • Improving application stability and player experience

Third-Party Services

Apes may use Meta platform services and related VR platform functionality required for online multiplayer and application distribution.

Data Sharing

FlipONER does not sell personal information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

Apes is not intended for users under the minimum age required by the Meta platform and applicable laws.
